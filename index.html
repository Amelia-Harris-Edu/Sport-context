YOUR WRITING CHALLENGE

Write a column arguing that great athletes have to be ruthless because fans only remember winners. Explore the true cost of success through the voice of a teammate who was left behind.

  • What has been left unsaid?
  • What would make this specific to Aotearoa, your community or your experience?
  • What could make it command attention?

IDEA LADDER: WATCH THE THINKING SHARPEN

1. OBVIOUS

Great athletes have to be ruthless.

2. MORE INTERESTING

Great athletes are praised for ruthlessness, but the people around them often pay the price.

3. SHARPER HOT TAKE

We call ruthless athletes champions because sport teaches us to value the result more than the damage.
